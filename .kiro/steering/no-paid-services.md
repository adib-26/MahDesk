# Paid services and accounts

- Never sign up for, subscribe to, upgrade, or start a trial of any paid service, plan, or API on the user's behalf without explicit permission in the current conversation.
- Never enter payment details or create accounts with third-party providers (email, hosting, SaaS, cloud, APIs).
- Before recommending or configuring a third-party provider, say whether it is free, has a free tier with limits, or is paid, and ask before proceeding with anything that could incur cost.
- Prefer free, local, or self-hosted options for development and testing (e.g. `MAIL_MAILER=log`, local stand-in servers, SQLite).
- Installing free open-source packages is fine; mention it when done.
